# GutBuster Gluttons – lorepakke (Peters hold)

Modtaget fra Peter 3. november 2026 (sammenfattet med Codex). Kilde til holdafsnit, pressekonferencer og Spike!-overskrifter.
Ny tekst om holdet skal følge **kanon-principperne** nederst. Teksten nedenfor er Peters egen.

**Peters rettelser 3. nov. (gælder før teksten nedenfor):**
- "Family Isn't Food" er **ikke officielt**. Brug det ikke på siden.
- Gnoblarer (Runts) **taler ikke** i pressekonferencer.
- **Runts' Union er uofficiel.** Nævn den ikke på siden. Gnoblarerne kan godt klage, men ikke som union.
- Runts er som Fraggle Rocks Doozers: De er der hele tiden, men ingen lægger mærke til dem, før de **ikke** er der.
  Ting "ordner sig selv", fordi Runts ordner dem i det skjulte.

GUTBUSTER GLUTTONS — TEAM LORE

## Team Identity
The Gutbuster Gluttons are an Ogre Blood Bowl team built around brute force, food, questionable administration and a surprisingly coherent internal culture.
They are not simply stupid Ogres who like eating.
The team functions because every part of the organisation follows its own strange logic:

* the Ogres solve problems through physical force;
* Coach Otto Overbelly solves problems through brutal practicality;
* the Gnoblars quietly perform most of the actual work;
* the league tries to impose normal bureaucracy;
* Spike! journalists desperately search for sophisticated explanations for behaviour that usually has a much simpler cause.

The result is an organisation that looks completely dysfunctional from the outside but somehow continues to operate.

## COACH OTTO OVERBELLY
Otto Overbelly is the heart of the Gutbuster Gluttons.
He is not merely stupid. Otto is:

* brutally practical;
* literal-minded;
* stubborn;
* opportunistic;
* magnificently cheap;
* unexpectedly competent by Ogre standards;
* completely sincere.

He rarely understands why anyone would complicate a problem that has an obvious solution.
His tactical philosophy can essentially be reduced to:
Procedure A: Hit them.
Procedure B: Hit them harder.
Procedure C: Throw a Gnoblar.
If none of that works: "Yer didn't hit 'em 'ard enough."

Otto does not normally blame his own players for defeat. Losses are caused by things such as:
Nuffle; referees; weather; statistics; slippery opponents; bad luck; insufficient hitting.

Otto especially distrusts statistics. One of his basic principles is: "Statistics don't hit people."
Journalists regularly mistake Otto's extremely simple observations for sophisticated strategic insight.
Otto will say: "We hit 'em."
A Spike! analyst may later describe this as: "A vertically integrated contact-pressure doctrine designed to collapse defensive spatial integrity."
Otto has no idea what that means.

## TEAM MOTTO
The official Gutbuster Gluttons motto is: "Winning is optional. Lunch is mandatory."
Other recurring team phrases include: "EAT. SMASH. REPEAT." / "Running Is Just Tenderizing." / "Family Isn't Food."

## OVERBELLY'S PRIME CUTS
Before becoming a Blood Bowl coach, Otto Overbelly operated a travelling butcher business called Overbelly's Prime Cuts.
The operation consisted of butcher wagons, meat, several Ogres, questionable bookkeeping and a population of Gnoblars living around, beneath and occasionally inside the business.
Overbelly's Prime Cuts eventually collapsed financially. Otto did not view this as failure. He simply repurposed the organisation.
The Ogres became Blood Bowl players. The Gnoblars became support staff. The butcher wagons became team infrastructure.
Thus the Gutbuster Gluttons were born.
The modern team still retains much of its butcher-shop heritage. The club often resembles a mixture of:
professional sports franchise; travelling butcher shop; field kitchen; workers' cooperative; health-and-safety violation.

## THE GUTPIT
The Gutbuster Gluttons play at The Gutpit.
The Gutpit is less a conventional stadium and more a Blood Bowl arena built around the remnants of a butcher yard, communal kitchen and Ogre encampment.
Common features include: enormous iron cooking pots; chopping blocks; meat hooks; battered sporting equipment; crude wooden signs; smoke from cooking fires; Gnoblar-built structures; grease-stained concessions; suspiciously industrial kitchens.
The Gutpit is chaotic, dangerous and badly regulated. It is also home.

## THE RUNTS' UNION
The Gnoblars are one of the most important parts of Gutbuster culture. Collectively, they are associated with the infamous Runts' Union.
Individually, Gnoblars are cowardly, chaotic and frequently unreliable. Collectively, they somehow keep the entire franchise alive.
The Runts handle much of the work involving: logistics; equipment; catering; paperwork; transport; injury reports; match administration; public relations; inventory; team communications.
The Ogres are officially the team. The Runts are effectively the civil service. This distinction is rarely acknowledged by management.
One recurring feature of Gutbuster history is Otto's complete inability to understand why the entire organisation stops functioning whenever the Runts threaten industrial action.

## GNOBLAR NAMING TRADITION
Ordinary Gutbuster Gnoblars traditionally do not receive proper names. They are identified with designations such as: Runt #1, Runt #4, Git #3.
A Gnoblar must earn the right to have a proper name. The traditional threshold is simple: A Gnoblar earns a name when he gains a skill.
In practical terms, surviving Blood Bowl long enough to become competent is what grants a Gnoblar personhood within team culture.
This is treated as completely normal. It is both a joke and a genuine Gutbuster tradition.

## RUNT #8
One famous historical Gnoblar became known simply as Runt #8.
Runt #8 achieved minor legendary status after somehow surviving long enough to run downfield and score.
Asked afterwards to explain the tactical brilliance involved, he reportedly said: "I ran. I kept running. I scored."
Sports journalists interpreted this as evidence of revolutionary offensive thinking. Otto did not.

## THROW TEAM-MATE SAFETY
The Runts' Union has repeatedly raised concerns regarding workplace safety. One particular issue dominates negotiations: Throw Team-Mate.
The Union regularly objects to Gnoblars being hurled across Blood Bowl pitches by Ogres.
Management regularly acknowledges these concerns. Management regularly continues throwing Gnoblars.
A typical sequence of events is: The Runts' Union submits a formal complaint. Otto reads or listens to part of it. A Gnoblar is thrown anyway.
The official position is that player safety remains a priority. No one has clearly defined whose priority.

## CANNIBALISM ALLEGATIONS
The Gutbuster Gluttons have repeatedly been accused of eating opposing players. The organisation categorically denies these accusations.
Opposing players occasionally disappear. Suspicious helmets sometimes appear near cooking pots. Pieces of armour occasionally turn up around butcher equipment.
The team maintains that these events are unrelated.
When questioned directly about cannibalism, Otto is genuinely offended. His attitude is best summarised by: "Good heavens, no. We're not savages!"
The allegations should never be definitively confirmed.
The humour comes from the fact that the evidence appears increasingly obvious while the Gluttons remain entirely sincere in their denial.

## FOOD ETHICS
Despite their reputation, the Gutbuster Gluttons have moral standards. One of the strongest rules within the organisation is: "Family Isn't Food."
Gnoblars belonging to the Gutbuster organisation are therefore regarded as family.
They may be: shouted at; thrown; trampled; used as administrative labour; subjected to catastrophic workplace conditions; but they are not considered lunch.
This creates an oddly wholesome moral boundary inside an otherwise terrifying organisation.

## HALFLINGS
Halflings occupy a peculiar place in Gutbuster philosophy. The Gluttons generally recognise them as:
excellent cooks; useful culinary consultants; entertaining companions; possibly pets; extremely valuable sources of snacks.
Consequently, Halflings are usually considered more useful alive than eaten. Exactly how firmly this principle is applied varies depending on circumstances.

## FOOD-RELATED EUPHEMISMS
"Officially, It's Just Pork." Used whenever the exact origin of meat would be inconvenient to discuss.
"Fast Food." A possible description of Skaven.
"Finger Food." A term occasionally associated with Halflings.
Official club representatives insist none of these expressions should be interpreted literally.

## OTTO'S ECONOMIC PHILOSOPHY
Otto is extremely cheap. He refers to this as: "Efficiency."
One of his guiding business principles is: "If one lad can do the job, don't send two. Two lads eat twice."
This philosophy affects almost every aspect of team management. Ogres are expensive capital assets. Gnoblars are operating expenditure. Food is an essential strategic resource. Almost everything else is negotiable.
Gutbuster accounting may therefore involve concepts such as: Ogre Capital Allocation; Meat Expenditure; Gnoblar Replacement Rate; Lunch-Time Performance; Throw Team-Mate Liability; Catering Contingency Reserves.
Otto takes such matters entirely seriously.

## TACTICAL PHILOSOPHY
Gutbuster tactics are fundamentally simple. Complexity is usually something added afterwards by journalists.
Core tactical concepts include:
Hitting – The preferred solution.
Hitting Harder – The preferred solution when hitting fails.
Throwing a Gnoblar – Used whenever hitting alone cannot solve the problem.
Area Denial – A term occasionally used by public relations when a badly thrown Gnoblar lands somewhere inconvenient.
Strategic Depth – Having another Ogre available to hit something.
Offensive Mobility – Throwing a Gnoblar towards the opposing end zone.
Defensive Adjustment – Hitting a different opponent.
The team occasionally possesses an official playbook. It is extremely short. There have also been persistent rumours that parts of it are actually cooking instructions.

## THE PLAYBOOK
1. Hit them.
2. Hit them harder.
3. Throw a Gnoblar.
4. Lunch.

No definitive evidence exists that Otto believes additional tactical theory is required.

## LUNCH
Lunch is one of the few genuinely sacred institutions within the Gutbuster Gluttons.
Winning is desirable. Lunch is mandatory. Training may stop for lunch. Tactical meetings may turn into lunch meetings.
Pantry shortages may be considered more serious than injury crises. The referee's whistle has occasionally been mistaken for the lunch signal.
Catering reports may receive more attention than match reports. Player compensation may partially consist of meat.
The team motto is not metaphorical.

## ASSISTANT COACH
The Gutbuster Gluttons have never maintained a particularly stable assistant-coaching structure.
The assistant coach is generally whichever Gnoblar is currently brave, unlucky or stupid enough to perform the role.
Turnover is high. Records are inconsistent. Some former assistant coaches are officially listed as: Missing.
This does not necessarily mean they are dead. The club prefers not to speculate.

## SPIKE! MAGAZINE
The Gutbuster Gluttons have a complicated relationship with sports journalism.
Spike! reporters are fascinated by the team because almost every Gutbuster event can be interpreted as either:
tactical genius; administrative incompetence; industrial dispute; public-relations disaster; criminal investigation; lunch.
Typical headlines may include:
"GUTBUSTERS LOSE. OTTO BLAMES WEATHER."
"RUNTS DEMAND SAFER WORKPLACE. MANAGEMENT ORDERS NEW CATAPULT."
"OVERBELLY DENIES CANNIBALISM FOR SIXTH CONSECUTIVE SEASON."
"RUNT #8 INVENTS RUNNING."
"GLUTTONS ANNOUNCE NEW TACTICAL SYSTEM. IT IS HITTING."
Spike! writers frequently interpret random Ogre behaviour as sophisticated sports strategy. Otto frequently corrects them. They rarely listen.

## PUBLIC RELATIONS
The Gutbuster public-relations department operates under permanent crisis conditions.
Recurring incidents include: missing paperwork; eaten questionnaires; unauthorized soup; suspicious catering incidents; assistant coaches disappearing; union complaints; damaged equipment; forms becoming lodged inside Ogre armour; contradictory press statements; missing opposition players; unexplained bones.
Despite this, the organisation continues functioning. Mostly because the Runts keep fixing things.

## INSTITUTIONAL CULTURE
The Gutbuster Gluttons are dysfunctional but not random. Their organisation has rules. Their rules are simply unusual.
Otto believes he runs a perfectly respectable Blood Bowl franchise.
The Ogres believe physical force is a legitimate universal problem-solving method.
The Gnoblars know that without them nothing would function.
The league believes paperwork can solve the situation.
Spike! believes there must be a deeper tactical explanation.
Everyone is wrong in slightly different ways. That is the core of Gutbuster humour.

## CANON PRINCIPLES
1. Otto Overbelly is sincere and internally logical.
2. Otto is cheap, but he is not deliberately cruel toward his own team.
3. Ogres solve problems primarily through force.
4. Gnoblars are treated as expendable Blood Bowl players but also as members of the Gutbuster family.
5. Family isn't food.
6. Gnoblars traditionally earn proper names through achievement.
7. The Runts' Union secretly keeps the organisation functioning.
8. Cannibalism is strongly implied but never officially confirmed.
9. Otto always sincerely denies cannibalism.
10. Journalists regularly mistake simple Ogre behaviour for advanced tactical thinking.
11. Lunch is genuinely important.
12. Gutbuster tactics should remain fundamentally simple.
13. Normal bureaucracy consistently fails when applied to Ogres.
14. The team should feel dysfunctional but strangely sustainable.
15. New jokes should grow naturally from existing lore rather than rely on random Ogre stupidity.
16. The humour should be dry, deadpan and internally consistent.
17. The Gutbusters should sometimes be unexpectedly wholesome, especially regarding their own Runts.
18. No matter how ridiculous the situation becomes, the characters themselves should generally take it seriously.

## THE CORE OF THE GUTBUSTER GLUTTONS
The Gutbuster Gluttons are not funny because they are incompetent.
They are funny because they have built a functioning institution around an Ogre understanding of the world.
Otto sincerely believes he is a responsible coach.
The Ogres sincerely believe hitting things is sound tactical doctrine.
The Runts sincerely believe somebody has to fill in the forms.
The league sincerely believes the forms matter.
And everyone agrees that lunch starts on time.
Winning is optional. Lunch is mandatory.
